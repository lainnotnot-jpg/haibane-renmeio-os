Haibane RenmeiOS 1.0 "Rakka"
═════════════════════════════
Arch-based · Hyprland · Shadow Shell · fastfetch

"Our wings are not for flying —
 they are for learning to walk."

A fan-made Arch Linux distribution themed after the
Haibane Renmei series. Ships with:

  • Hyprland (Wayland) + SDDM login
  • Shadow Shell (DarkShadowShell v3.60) — Material 3 bar,
    launcher, dock, widgets, lock screen & Settings app
  • fastfetch preloaded with the Haibane Renmei logo
  • Preinstalled Haibane Renmei wallpapers (system-wide in
    /usr/share/haibane-renmei-os/wallpapers/ and in
    ~/Pictures/Wallpapers/), shown by hyprpaper on first login
  • PipeWire audio, NetworkManager, the fish shell

Project: https://github.com/lainnotnot-jpg/haibane-renmeio-os
